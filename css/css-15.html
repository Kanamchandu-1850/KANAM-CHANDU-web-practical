overflow, overflow-x, overflow-y

Overflow Handling

Demonstrates hidden, scroll, and horizontal-only overflow on fixed-size boxes.

overflow:hidden — extra content is clipped and not shown. This sentence is intentionally long to prove the point.
overflow:scroll — always shows scrollbars, even if content fits, so you can scroll to see this whole sentence.
overflow-x:auto with nowrap — scrolls sideways only: one long line of text that keeps going and going.
